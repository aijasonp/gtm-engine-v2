# BDR MEETING SPEC — {{COMPANY}} BDR Team
_Last updated: 2026-10-03 (per {{AE_2}} thread)_

## GOAL
The BDR's job is to BOOK qualified meetings. BDRs are measured on meetings booked. The BDR owns the first meeting themselves (discovery). AEs only enter after the BDR qualifies the prospect.

## DEFINITION: BOOKED MEETING
A meeting is BOOKED the moment the prospect agrees to a specific meeting at a specific time/place. Agreement in writing (LinkedIn, email, text) counts. A calendar invite is NOT required.

**Examples of booked:**
- "Let's meet after the breakfast" (specific event, specific slot)
- "Sure, Tuesday at 2pm works"
- "Yes, send me an invite"

**Not booked:**
- "Find me at the conference" (no commitment)
- "Maybe sometime next month"
- Pleasantries with no time or place attached

## CALENDAR INVITES
Not required for a meeting to count. Do not add friction to a yes. If the prospect agrees in a LinkedIn thread, confirm logistics in that thread. Offer your own contact info instead of asking for theirs. Only send a calendar invite if the prospect volunteers their email or asks for one.

## HELD VS BOOKED
Booked is the BDR's metric. Held rate (did the meeting actually happen) is tracked at the team level and is a quality signal, not the BDR's primary score. Protect held rate by confirming tight logistics (specific time, specific spot), not by doing AE work.

## WHO THE MEETING IS WITH
BDR meetings are with the BDR. That is the intended flow. The prospect meeting the BDR first is the job, not a downgrade.

Flow:
1. BDR books the meeting (counts immediately)
2. BDR takes the meeting (15-30 min discovery, coffee or call)
3. BDR qualifies: fit, pain, authority, timeline
4. If qualified, BDR books the NEXT meeting with an AE (handoff)
5. If not qualified, log learnings and move on

## SUBSTANTIVE TEST
A held meeting is substantive if you can write 2-3 sentences afterward about what the prospect cares about. A 2-minute hallway handshake does not count as held.

## LOGGING
Log every booked meeting in HubSpot at booking time:
- Source (LinkedIn InMail, event, cold call, etc.)
- Date, time, location or format
- Evidence (screenshot or link to the written agreement)
- Company and prospect role
- After the meeting: held / no-show, plus discovery notes
